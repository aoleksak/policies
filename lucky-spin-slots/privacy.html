Privacy Policy — Lucky Spin: Vegas Nights

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Lucky Spin: Vegas Nights ("the app") is a free-to-play casino-style entertainment game published by HA Holdings, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app does — and does not — handle.

Summary

We collect no personal data ourselves. The app has no user accounts and no analytics. Everything the game saves (credits, level progress, settings) is stored only on your device. The app offers optional rewarded ads, served by Google AdMob, which you can choose to watch for bonus credits; Google collects some device data to serve those ads (see below).

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share any personal information. The only network connections the app makes are those of Google Play (purchases) and Google AdMob (ads).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads appear only when you choose to watch one, for example to double a free gift or a bonus win. The app never shows ads you did not ask for. To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads load, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Ads in the app are limited to content rated for teens and older.

Data stored on your device

Game state — your credit balance, experience level, daily-bonus timers, settings, and similar gameplay data — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details, name, or email address. Google's handling of your payment information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The app stores only anonymous purchase tokens on your device to prevent purchases from being lost or double-counted.

Children

The app contains simulated gambling and is intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Simulated gambling disclaimer

Lucky Spin: Vegas Nights is for entertainment only. Virtual credits have no real-world value, cannot be exchanged for money or prizes, and cannot be cashed out. The app offers no real-money gambling and no opportunity to win anything of value. Success at this game does not imply future success at real-money gambling.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised before the change takes effect.
