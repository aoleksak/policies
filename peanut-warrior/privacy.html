Privacy Policy — Peanut Warrior

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Peanut Warrior ("the game") is a free action game for Android published by HA Holdings, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game does — and does not — handle.

Summary

The game asks for no personal information. It has no user accounts and no servers of ours. Everything the game saves (coins, upgrades, unlocked heroes, settings) is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, also in your own Play Games account so you can carry it to a new phone. The only third parties involved are Google's Play Games, analytics, advertising and billing services described below. Anonymous usage statistics can be turned off at any time, and ads are shown only when you choose to watch one.

Data stored on your device

Your progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game, or using Settings > Reset progress, deletes this local copy.

Google Play Games (achievements and cloud save)

On Android the game uses Google Play Games Services. If you have a Play Games profile, Google signs you in automatically. While you are signed in, the game unlocks achievements in your Play Games profile and saves a copy of your game progress (the same coins, upgrades and unlocks kept on your device) to your Play Games account as a saved game. We cannot see this data: it is stored by Google, linked to your Google account, and used only to restore your progress on another device. Google's handling of it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete the game's saved data and achievements from the Google Play Games app or your Google account's data settings.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

On Android the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played so we can improve it: for example which screens and reward offers are used, how long runs last and how they end, and which items are bought. With these events Google collects an app-instance identifier, basic device information (such as model, operating system version, language and approximate country) and app-version details. We do not send your name, email address, contacts or precise location, and we use the statistics only in aggregate. Because the game's ads come from Google AdMob, Google may also use this data to measure those ads; in regions where ad consent is required (see below), only if you have consented. You can turn usage statistics off at any time in Settings > Share usage stats. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Reminders (notifications)

If you allow notifications, the game can schedule at most one or two local reminders a day (for example when the Daily Chest is ready or your scouts are back). They are created on your device; no data is sent anywhere to show them. Turn them off in Settings > Reminders or in your device's notification settings.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The game can show short video ads served by Google AdMob, and only when you tap a button that offers one in exchange for an in-game reward (for example a revive or bonus coins). There are no forced ads: nothing plays between runs or when the game opens.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, and information about how you interact with ads. In regions with privacy laws (the EEA, the UK and some US states) a consent notice is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. If you decline, the game simply does not offer ads. How Google uses this data is described in the Google advertising policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details, name or email address; the game receives only an anonymous purchase token and the product ID so it can grant the item. Google's handling of your payment information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The game keeps the purchase tokens on your device to prevent purchases from being lost or double-counted.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Never tap a "Watch ad" button and no ad, and no ad-related data collection, occurs.
  • Turn off usage statistics in Settings > Share usage stats, and reminders in Settings > Reminders.
  • Change or withdraw ad consent in Settings > Privacy options.
  • Reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your device's Google settings.
  • Delete local game data by uninstalling the game; delete the cloud copy and achievements from the Google Play Games app.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised before the change takes effect.
